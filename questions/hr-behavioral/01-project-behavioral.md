# 🎯 HR & Behavioral — Interview Questions

> Common behavioral/managerial questions asked across all companies.

---

## Q1. Explain Your Project Architecture and Your Role in It

### 📝 One-Liner
Walk through your system's architecture and articulate your specific contributions and responsibilities.

### 🔑 Quick Answer
Use the STAR framework: Situation (project context + scale), Task (your role), Action (technical decisions you drove), Result (impact + metrics). *(STAR framework use karo — Situation, Task, Action, Result)*

### 📖 How It Works

**Structure your answer**:
1. **Project Overview** (30 sec): What does the system do? Who uses it? Scale?
2. **Architecture** (1 min): Draw/describe the high-level architecture
   - Services, databases, message queues, caches
3. **Your Role** (1 min): What did YOU specifically own?
   - Features you built
   - Design decisions you made
   - Problems you solved
4. **Impact** (30 sec): Quantifiable results
   - Performance improvements (e.g., "reduced latency by 40%")
   - Business impact (e.g., "processed 2M transactions/day")

### 🗣️ How to Say in Interview
"I work on a [domain] platform that handles [X requests/day]. The architecture consists of [describe 3-4 key services]. I specifically own the [service name] which handles [responsibility]. A key design decision I made was [choosing X over Y because Z]. This resulted in [quantifiable improvement]."

### ⚠️ Pitfalls / Gotchas
- Don't use "we" for everything — highlight YOUR contributions *(apna contribution clearly batao)*
- Don't just describe — explain WHY decisions were made
- Be ready for deep-dives: "Why did you choose Kafka over RabbitMQ?"
- Don't share confidential/proprietary information

### ⚡ Remember
- Prepare 2-3 projects at different complexity levels
- Know exact numbers: RPS, latency, data volume
- Always mention trade-offs considered
- Connect relevance to the interviewing company's domain
